About SovereigntyAI

Autonomous Cloud Data Sovereignty Compliance Framework for Cross-Border Cloud Applications.

Project Overview & College Course Objectives

SovereigntyAI is a college capstone software project designed to solve a pressing dilemma in modern cloud engineering: how multinational enterprises can store and transfer digital records across public cloud regions (such as Amazon Web Services) while rigorously adhering to contradictory, extraterritorial data sovereignty laws like the European Union GDPR, the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), and US healthcare/financial regulations (HIPAA/PCI-DSS).

The framework couples a deterministic, rule-based policy intelligence engine with an explainable AI (XAI) risk-scoring model and an advisory scikit-learn machine learning baseline. It evaluates cloud storage configurations, explains the exact reasoning behind every compliance decision, calculates a calibrated exposure score from 0 to 100, and recommends actionable AWS remediations.

1. Deterministic Policy Intelligence

Unlike black-box generative AI models that can hallucinate legal advice, SovereigntyAI executes formal condition trees grounded in statutory text (e.g. GDPR Chapter V Articles 44-49, DPDP Act 2023 Sections 8 & 16). Every evaluation outcome is linked to statutory citations and official regulatory guidelines.

2. Explainable Risk Algorithm (XAI)

The 0-100 risk score is completely transparent. It begins with data sensitivity baseline weights (e.g. PHI / biometrics = 40), adds statutory penalty points for missing transfer mechanisms (+25) or missing encryption (+15), and applies mitigation credits for defense-in-depth security controls (KMS encryption: -10, IAM RBAC: -8, CloudTrail logging: -7).

3. Enterprise AWS Architecture

Designed for frictionless extension to AWS: Amazon S3 for immutable audit storage, Amazon RDS PostgreSQL for enterprise persistence, AWS Lambda for event-driven policy evaluation, Amazon SNS for compliance breach notifications, and Amazon CloudWatch for telemetry.

Viva Voce Quick Cheat-Sheet

Q: Why not use a pure Large Language Model (LLM) or deep neural net for legal decisions?
A: Regulatory compliance mandates 100% determinism, zero hallucinations, non-repudiation, and auditability. A rule engine guarantees consistency; our ML component acts purely as an advisory heuristic trained on synthetic benchmark data.
Q: Does GDPR prohibit all cross-border transfers outside the EU?
A: No. GDPR Chapter V permits international transfers if an Adequacy Decision (Article 45) exists, or if Appropriate Safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46 SCCs) with supplementary technical measures (EDPB 01/2020) are in place.
Q: What is the cross-border transfer model under India's DPDP Act 2023?
A: Section 16 of the DPDP Act 2023 adopts a "blacklist" or "negative-list" approach: personal data transfers outside India are permitted by default, except to countries restricted by the Central Government.
